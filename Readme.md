## Valar Group, Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL, and Zcash Foundation Updates

- **NU7 is live.** The network upgrade reduced the target block time from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. The five Zcash development organizations coordinated on the upgrade. [Read the November 2026 State of Encrypted Money report](https://www.coindesk.com/research/zcash-monthly-state-of-encrypted-money-november-2026-edition).

- **Shielded Labs launched Epoch.** Epoch is a research unit focused on protection against future quantum-computing threats. [Read the report](https://www.coindesk.com/research/zcash-monthly-state-of-encrypted-money-november-2026-edition).

- **Ecosystem funding was announced.** On November 10, 2026, Grayscale announced grants of $100,000 each to the Zcash Foundation, Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, and Pretty Good Policy for Zcash. [Read the announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399445/0/en/grayscale-launches-zcash-investor-campaign.html).

- **Zakura released version 1.8.8 as a preview.** The preview was published on November 14, 2026. [View the Zakura release](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/releases/tag/v1.8.8).

- **ZODL community link.** Use the [ZODL project page on the Zcash ecosystem directory](https://openzcash.org/ecosystem) to verify the latest release and activity before publication.

- **Zcash Foundation.** The Foundation is included in the November ecosystem funding announcement above. Check the [Zcash Foundation website](https://zfnd.org/) for any update published between November 17 and November 21.

- **Project Tachyon and Valar Group.** Check the [Zcash ecosystem directory](https://openzcash.org/ecosystem) and the [Zcash Community Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/) for the latest project updates before publication. No additional activity is stated here without a verified source link.


## Zcash Community Grants

- **November 9 committee meeting minutes.** Zcash Community Grants published minutes from its November 9 meeting. [Read the meeting minutes](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-11-9-2026/59204).
